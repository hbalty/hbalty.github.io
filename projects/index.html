C'Diffusion

Apr 2017 – Aug 2017
ClientEnvironmentStatus
CdiscountSymfony, ReactJs, Ansible, MS SQL, Azure server, Azure AD, SAMLproduction

C’diffusion is a web application dedicated to Cdiscount teams to report any incident that can occur in the company’s infrastructure. I was in charge of designing and developing this application to meet the support team's needs. Agility, precision and availability were the main challenges.

Its main purpose is to keep track of all incidents that are happening or have happened in the company, allowing teams to measure the risk of a change. It also notifies anyone impacted by an incident in real time using text messages or emails.

To keep the application available even during an incident on the local network, it was hosted on Microsoft Azure and users log in with their Azure AD credentials.
